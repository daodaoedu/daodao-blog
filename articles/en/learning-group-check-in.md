## What will the group discuss?

A teacher or community leader can prepare one question, such as: “What learning challenge would you like to work through with the group this week?”

1. Introduce the discussion topic.
2. Explain how participants can respond.
3. Leave time for participants to add questions.

| Preparation | Example |
| --- | --- |
| Topic | A learning challenge from this week |
| Participation | One question from each person |

These are examples of session preparation, not product outcome promises or verified research findings.
